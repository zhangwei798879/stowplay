Stowplay

A local-first cabinet for your own game files and saves. Pair the app with a Local Agent on your computer or NAS to browse your library, verify files, move copies between storage, and back up and restore saves with read-back checks.

  • No developer account or cloud service — your data stays on your devices.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Works with files you already own; it does not download or provide games.
